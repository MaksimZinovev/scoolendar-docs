# Getting Started

This guide walks you through the basics of using Scoolendar for the first time.

## 1. Create your account

Scoolendar uses a simple username and password system. Create your account and sign in at [scoolendar.com](https://scoolendar.com).

## 2. Set up your children's learning

Start by telling Scoolendar about each child you are homeschooling. This keeps each child's curriculum, timetable and progress separate.

## 3. Create a curriculum

A curriculum describes **what** your child is learning — the subjects and topics you plan to cover. Begin with just one or two subjects; you can expand it later.

## 4. Build a timetable

A timetable describes **when** learning happens — how your week is structured. Place your curriculum topics into the week so you have a clear plan at a glance.

## 5. Track progress

As your child completes lessons and activities, record their progress. Over time this builds a picture of their learning, which is helpful for planning — and for registration evidence.

## Tips for getting started

- Start simple: one child, a few subjects, one week of timetable.
- Iterate: adjust the timetable as you learn what works for your family.
- Regular small updates beat big catch-up sessions.
