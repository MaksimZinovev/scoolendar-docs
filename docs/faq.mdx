# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need to install anything?

No. Scoolendar is a web-based tool — everything works in your browser at [scoolendar.com](https://scoolendar.com).

## Who is Scoolendar for?

Homeschooling families — especially parents organising their children's education at home. It is currently used mostly by families in Australia, and is growing to families around the world.

## How do I sign in?

Scoolendar uses a simple username and password.

## Where is my data kept?

Scoolendar keeps your data in Australia.

## How are payments handled?

Payments are processed securely by [Stripe](https://stripe.com).

## Is it hard to use?

Scoolendar is designed to be simple and easy to use. See [Getting Started](/getting-started) for the basics, and ask the **Ask AI** panel whenever you are stuck.

## How do I get more help?

- Ask the **Ask AI** panel (sparkle icon in the header) — it answers from these help pages.
- Learn more about the project at [scoolendar.com/about](https://scoolendar.com/about).
