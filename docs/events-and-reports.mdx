# Events & Registration Reports

Scoolendar helps you discover educational activities near you and turn them into evidence for your homeschooling registration.

## Finding local events

Browse the events section to find local events and educational activities posted by other families and groups. Events are regularly added for homeschooling families.

## Posting an event

Running something other families would love? You can post your own local event so others can find it.

## Recording attendance

When you attend an event, you can record it as an attended event against your child's learning.

## Creating a summary report

Attended events can be turned into a **summary report**. Homeschooling families typically use this when **applying for homeschooling registration**, as it documents the educational activities your child has taken part in.

### How families use it

1. Attend an event (excursion, workshop, group activity).
2. Record the attendance in Scoolendar.
3. Generate the summary report when you are preparing your registration application or evidence.

## Community Connect

Looking for more activities? [Community Connect](/community-connect) connects you with local homeschooling groups that often run their own events.
