# Welcome to Scoolendar Help

Scoolendar ([scoolendar.com](https://scoolendar.com)) is a web-based tool for homeschooling families. It helps parents organise their children's education by creating timetables and tracking learning progress.

## What can you do with Scoolendar?

- **Curriculum and timetables** — create a curriculum for each child and build a timetable that shows how their week is structured.
- **Track learning progress** — keep a record of what has been covered over time.
- **Community Connect** — connect with other local homeschooling groups.
- **Local events** — post or find local events and educational activities in your area.
- **Registration reports** — turn attended events into a summary report, which is useful when applying for homeschooling registration.
- **Resource library** — a library of useful links and resources for homeschooling families.

## Who is it for?

Scoolendar is designed for homeschooling families — parents who manage their children's education at home. It is built to be simple and easy to use, with regular updates and new events posted for the community.
