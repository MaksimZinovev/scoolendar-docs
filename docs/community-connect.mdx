# Community Connect

Community Connect is where Scoolendar helps you find your local homeschooling community.

## What it does

- **Find local groups** — discover other homeschooling families and groups near you.
- **Coordinate together** — connect with groups to share activities, excursions and support.

## Why it matters

Homeschooling works best with community. Local groups give your family social connection, shared learning activities, and experienced parents who have answered the same questions you are asking.

## Next steps

- Browse [local events](/events-and-reports) posted by groups and families.
- Post an event of your own once you are part of a group.
